Our Story

EST. 2026 • FROM A HUMBLE HOME KITCHEN

To be honest, I absolutely hated peanut butter. I found it too oily, and the taste felt completely artificial. That all changed when I tried a true craft brand and realized real peanut butter only needs two ingredients.

I thought to myself: If it only takes two ingredients, why can't I make it myself?

That sparked an obsession. I started roasting and grinding my own batches from a small, humble home kitchen, falling in love with the process. Now, I eat it almost every day for breakfast—at least three times a week!

"If it's not healthy enough for my kitchen, it's not going into yours."

— My Sister

I present to you the peanut butter I make and love. For the beautiful handful of you who picked our small business just starting out, you can finally taste this simple, pure elixir.

The Grounded Promise:

  • Hydrogenated Vegetable Oil
  • Palm Oil
  • Refined Sugar
  • Preservatives
  • Emulsifiers
