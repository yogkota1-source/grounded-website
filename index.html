Our Story

EST. 2026 • THE GROUNDED STANDARD

The Art of Subtraction

If you read the label on a standard supermarket jar, you are usually met with a dense paragraph of stabilizers, emulsifiers, and refined syrups. Grounded was born from the exact opposite approach. Take our Simply Seeded peanut butter. Instead of engineering a complex formula, we rely on the quiet power of three pure elements: deeply roasted peanuts, a pinch of Himalayan pink salt, and your choice of a clean, natural sweetener—monk fruit or date powder. It is an exercise in letting real food speak for itself.

The Luxury of Patience

But pure ingredients only matter if the process respects them. We believe the highest form of trust is transparency in time. The truth is, a jar of Simply Seeded doesn't actually exist until you ask for it. We rejected the traditional warehouse model to run strictly on micro-batches. Your jar is roasted, ground, and packed only after your order drops into our system. It demands a little more patience from both of us, but the reward is opening a jar at its absolute peak of freshness.

The Bigger Blueprint

This standard is not just a rule for our peanut butter—it is the blueprint for our entire kitchen. We are currently applying this exact philosophy to our upcoming lines of wild-fermented Kimchi (ĀCHAR) and slow-brewed Kombucha (Soma). Whether we are crafting a sweet spread, a complex spicy ferment, or a botanical elixir, the promise remains uncompromised: fewer ingredients, better sweeteners, and food crafted with absolute integrity.
