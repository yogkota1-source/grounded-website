Privacy Policy

How we collect, process, and safeguard your personal data.

3.1 Introduction & Legal Compliance

Welcome to Grounded. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your information when you browse our website, authenticate your account, chat with our AI assistants, or purchase our consumables. This policy is drafted in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 (along with the IT Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures Rules, 2011) of India. By using our website, you provide your free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent to the data practices described below.

3.2 The "Zero Data Selling" Guarantee

Grounded will never sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers, ad networks, or external marketing agencies. Your information is used strictly to authenticate your session, fulfill and deliver your orders, provide customer support, and secure our platform against fraud.

3.3 Personal Data We Collect & Purpose Limitation

We practice strict data minimization and collect only the information required to operate our store safely:

  • Identity & Delivery Data: First name, last name, email address, phone number, physical shipping address, and PIN code. Purpose: To verify account identity, validate order authenticity, print courier shipping labels, and coordinate physical delivery.
  • Authentication & Security Data: Unique user identifiers (UIDs), authentication tokens (via Google Sign-In / Firebase), IP addresses, and rate-limit counters. Purpose: To keep your session secure, prevent unauthorized access, and block automated spam or abuse on our checkout and AI endpoints.
  • Transaction Data: Items ordered, order totals, Razorpay Order IDs, Payment IDs, and cryptographic verification signatures. Purpose: Order fulfillment, statutory accounting, tax compliance, and customer support.
  • AI Chat Interaction Data: Text prompts submitted to our AI assistants (TC, TR, TA). Purpose: To generate real-time answers to your product questions. Chat prompts are processed transiently and are not used to build advertising profiles.

3.4 Financial Data Isolation & Payment Security

Grounded does not collect, view, or store your raw credit card numbers, debit card credentials, netbanking passwords, or UPI PINs.

All payment transactions are executed directly on the encrypted servers of our RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS compliant payment processor (Razorpay).

Grounded only receives a payment confirmation token and cryptographic signature to verify whether your transaction succeeded.

3.5 Trusted Third-Party Data Processors

To operate a secure, modern e-commerce platform, we share strictly necessary data subsets with the following categories of vetted service providers:

  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay (to authorize payments, detect payment fraud, and process approved refunds).
  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Delivery providers (such as BlueDart, Delhivery, India Post, or local hyperlocal couriers) who require your name, address, PIN code, and phone number to deliver your package.
  • Cloud, Authentication & AI Infrastructure:
    • Cloudflare (Content delivery, DDoS protection, serverless API execution, and anti-spam KV rate-limiting).
    • Google Firebase (Secure user authentication and session token verification).
    • Google Workspace / Sheets (Encrypted, automated internal order fulfillment logging).
    • Google Gemini API (Processing customer questions sent to our storefront AI assistants).

All operational partners process data solely to perform their designated technical or logistical function for Grounded.

3.6 Cookies, Local Storage & Consent Management

Our website uses strictly necessary first-party browser storage (localStorage and session cookies) to:

  • Remember the items and quantities in your shopping cart across pages;
  • Maintain your authenticated login session during checkout; and
  • Record your acceptance of our legal terms and privacy consent gate.

We do not use invasive third-party advertising pixels to track your behavior across unrelated websites. Disabling browser storage or cookies may prevent the cart and checkout system from functioning.

3.7 Data Security & Retention

Security Safeguards: We enforce HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, strict Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) policies, server-side input sanitization, and HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.

Retention Period: We retain order and transaction records only for as long as necessary to fulfill your order, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and comply with mandatory Indian tax and accounting laws (typically up to 8 financial years where required by statute). Security rate-limit logs in our edge cache automatically expire after 24 hours.

3.8 Your Rights as a Data Principal (Under DPDPA 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact or account details.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data from our active systems, unless retention is legally mandated for completed financial transactions, tax audits, or active dispute resolution.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for future data processing at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer (note that withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal & Nomination: Register a complaint with our Data Protection Officer and nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
