Refund, Replacement & Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy carefully before placing an order.

1.1 Strict Zero-Return Policy for Perishable Consumables

All items sold by Grounded (including Simply Seeded and Āchar collections) are perishable, consumable food products handcrafted in a home kitchen. Due to strict health, hygiene, and FSSAI food safety regulations, we operate an absolute zero-return policy across our entire catalog.

We will never accept a physical return or arrange a reverse courier pickup under any circumstances.

This applies even if the package is unopened, the tamper seal is intact, or the shipping box was never opened. Do not ship items back to us. Any returned food items will be immediately destroyed upon arrival for safety and hygiene reasons, and no refund will be issued.

1.2 Mandatory 48-Hour Reporting Window

We do not manually monitor individual courier tracking links after dispatch. It is your sole responsibility to receive and inspect your package immediately upon delivery.

If there is a transit breakage, missing item, or wrong item delivered, you must contact our official support email or WhatsApp strictly within 48 hours from the exact timestamp the courier marks the package as "Delivered."

Any claim submitted outside this 48-hour window is invalid and will be rejected without exception.

1.3 Transit Damage vs. Post-Delivery Drops (The Mandatory Unboxing Video Rule)

We cover jars that are shattered or damaged by the courier during transit. We do not cover jars that are accidentally dropped or mishandled by the customer after delivery. To prevent fraudulent or staged claims, we enforce a strict verification protocol:

Mandatory Continuous Unboxing Video: To claim a replacement or refund for transit damage, missing items, or wrong items, you must provide a single, continuous, unedited unboxing video (without cuts, pauses, or splices) that clearly shows:

  • The sealed, unopened outer shipping box from all angles with the shipping label clearly legible;
  • The breaking of the outer tape/tamper seal and removal of the internal protective packaging; and
  • The damaged product along with the printed Batch Code on the jar.

The Outer Box & Supporting Photos: In addition to the unboxing video, you must provide clear photographs of the damaged outer shipping box, shipping label, and broken jar.

Automatic Rejection: Static photographs alone (without the continuous unboxing video showing the package being opened for the first time) are insufficient proof, as damage can occur after unpacking. If the outer shipping box was discarded or you cannot provide the unboxing video, the incident will be classified as post-delivery handling damage and the claim will be rejected.

1.4 Spoilage, Expiry, and Improper Customer Storage

Our consumables are 100% natural and contain zero artificial preservatives, synthetic stabilizers, or chemical anti-fungal agents.

Customer Storage Duty: It is the customer's sole responsibility to read and follow the storage instructions printed on the packaging (e.g., refrigerating fermented foods immediately upon arrival, keeping nut butters away from moisture and direct sunlight, and exclusively using a clean, dry spoon at all times).

Zero Liability for Post-Delivery Contamination: Introducing even a drop of water, saliva, or food residue via a wet or used spoon will cause natural food to mold rapidly. Grounded holds zero liability for mold, spoilage, or degradation caused by improper storage, heat exposure, or utensil cross-contamination after delivery.

1.5 Natural Characteristics: Oil Separation & Live Fermentation

Because we craft real, whole foods without synthetic additives, natural physical and biological changes occur during transit:

  • No Refunds for Subjective Taste: We do not offer refunds, replacements, or exchanges based on personal taste preferences, spice tolerance, tanginess, sweetness levels, or texture.
  • Nut Butter Oil Separation (Simply Seeded): Because we refuse to use hydrogenated oils or synthetic emulsifiers, natural nut and seed oils will separate and pool at the top of the jar during transit. This is the hallmark of pure nut butter, not a defect. Stir thoroughly from the bottom before consuming.
  • Live Probiotic Fermentation (Āchar): Our wild-fermented products (such as Kimchi) contain live, unpasteurized lactic acid cultures that remain active during transit. Natural carbonation (fizzing/bubbling), slight lid pressure, minor brine weeping around the seal due to warm transit temperatures, strong pungent aromas, and increasing sourness over time are completely normal biological traits of living food—not spoilage or defects. Always chill fermented jars in the refrigerator for 4–6 hours upon arrival before opening slowly over a sink.
  • Artisanal Fill Weights: All jars are packed by weight, not volume. Settling during transit or natural density variations (within permissible Legal Metrology limits) do not constitute a weight shortage.

1.6 Accurate Shipping Details & Undeliverable Orders (RTO)

You are legally and financially responsible for providing a complete, real, and accurate legal name, street address, serviceable 6-digit Indian PIN code, and active phone number at checkout.

Fictitious or Incomplete Details: If you enter placeholder text, aliases, incomplete addresses, or invalid PIN codes, or if our verification systems flag an order as fraudulent or unserviceable, we reserve the right to hold or cancel the order.

Failed Deliveries & Return to Origin (RTO): Because our products are perishable foods that degrade during extended courier transit, if an order cannot be delivered and is marked "Return to Origin" (RTO) or delayed due to: (a) an incorrect, fake, or incomplete address provided by you, (b) your phone being switched off or unreachable when the delivery partner calls, or (c) your refusal to accept the package upon arrival, no refund or free replacement will be issued.

1.7 Cancellations & Refund Processing Timeline

  • Cancellations: Orders may only be canceled within 2 hours of placement by contacting support. Once batch preparation, packing, or courier manifest generation has begun, orders cannot be canceled or modified.
  • Refund Timeline: If a refund is approved under these terms, it will be initiated to your original payment method and will reflect within 7 to 10 business days (excluding weekends and banking holidays), subject to your bank’s processing timelines.
